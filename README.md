A long-only cbBTC (Coinbase Wrapped BTC) trading bot for make.fun's
[Race a BTC trading bot live for 24 hours](https://make.fun/bounty/btc-race-24h-20261123-0511)
bounty. It trades 1-minute on-chain bars from an Orca Whirlpool pool with
$1,000 of paper money. The dev window's +3.6% came as two sharp jumps
followed by flat chop, and every fast trend rule tested lost money to
whipsaw and ~26–46 bps round-trip costs — so this bot buys the full balance
on the first bar and holds, protected only by a fixed 1.5% stop-loss on the
position's cost, with no re-entry. On the dev window it nets +$32.17 with a
1.98% max drawdown; on a gradual 5% decline it stops out at about −$18
instead of riding the full drop.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's 'Race a BTC trading bot live for 24 hours' bounty, $50 in USDC.
